# AGENTS_CORPUS

Pointer into the Observatory's map. Do not hand-edit.

`corpus/` is generated by `python -m map_agents --root corpus <command>` and is not present in a
fresh checkout until the first write. Once it exists, the live pointer is
[`corpus/AGENTS_CORPUS.md`](corpus/AGENTS_CORPUS.md), rebuilt by `... build`; start there, or at
[`corpus/map/index.md`](corpus/map/index.md).

For how to look something up, record a lead, or run a bounded refresh, read
[`skills/map-the-agents/SKILL.md`](skills/map-the-agents/SKILL.md).
