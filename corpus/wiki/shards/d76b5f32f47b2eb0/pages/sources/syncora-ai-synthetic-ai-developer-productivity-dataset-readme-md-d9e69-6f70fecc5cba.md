- Suggested applications include ML training for productivity forecasting, time tracking and optimization algorithm design, burnout detection research, and synthetic benchmarking for HR and engineering analytics tools. [@claim:clm_a196e6acf6b38ed34c09a53d3a17eef2d4471ce58d48636aee99c0aab893dd8c]
