## Description

Highlight (site page `what_makes_it_special`): Y Combinator-backed platform that turns Claude or ChatGPT into a full app builder with backend, database, hosting, logins, and one-click publishing; available as a ChatGPT plugin and MCP connector.

(captured site page body (agents/floot.md), not a verified repo-code finding)
Floot, a Y Combinator-backed startup, bet that the AI assistant a user already talks to is the right interface for app building — so instead of a separate studio, it connects as an MCP connector and the assistant's conversation becomes the build session. Underneath, the platform handles what non-developers usually fail at: hosting at yourapp.floot.app, database, user accounts and logins, recurring tasks, transactional email, and SEO defaults, with Stripe, Google login, and Zapier integrations behind guided setup. Publishing is one click, and refinement happens by continuing the conversation rather than editing code. Pricing is flat plans rather than per-token credits, with credits reserved for the optional Floot agent and image generation, which targets founders and operators shipping internal tools and MVPs without writing code.
