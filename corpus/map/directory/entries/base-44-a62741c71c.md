Base44 is an AI app-building platform aimed at people who want working software without managing infrastructure. Users describe an app, website, or agent in plain language, and the platform generates the full stack - frontend, backend, database, authentication, payments, and hosting - with no deployment step. Iteration happens conversationally, and built-in SEO/GEO dashboards, analytics, and AI-generated marketing content address the go-to-market side that pure coding tools skip. Superagents extend the platform by connecting to user tools and automating workflows inside built apps. The platform targets non-developers and small businesses building production apps without touching code, with a free tier plus paid plans and enterprise options.
