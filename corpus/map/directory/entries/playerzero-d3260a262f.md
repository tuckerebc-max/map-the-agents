## Description

Highlight (site page `what_makes_it_special`): AI that connects product defects to code changes and fixes

(captured site page body (agents/playerzero.md), not a verified repo-code finding)
PlayerZero attacks the gap between where defects surface (production, support tickets, incident channels) and where they are understood (the codebase), maintaining an engineering world model that joins code, tickets, observability signals, ownership, and deploy history into one queryable structure. Agents on the platform triage incoming issues, run root-cause analysis, delegate fixes to the responsible owners, and validate the results, with each resolution feeding back into the model as institutional memory. The company's SIM-1 research models simulate large codebases and infrastructure, fanning out parallel debugging scenarios or pre-release verification runs as a release gate. Customer-reported outcomes include Zuora cutting L3 triage from three days to fifteen minutes and First Street reducing MTTR by 98%. The platform targets engineering, support, SRE, and QA teams at enterprises (SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, ISO 42001), sold through demo-led sales rather than public pricing.
