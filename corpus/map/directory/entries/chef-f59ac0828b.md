## Description

Highlight (site page `what_makes_it_special`): AI app builder by Convex (fork of bolt.diy) that generates full-stack web apps with real backend — built-in database, zero-config auth, file uploads, real-time UIs, and background workflows via Convex APIs. Described as 'the only AI app builder that knows backend.' Hosted at chef.convex.dev with a generous free tier.

(captured site page body (agents/chef.md), not a verified repo-code finding)
Chef is Convex's answer to a specific gap in AI app builders: they generate frontends fluently but leave the backend as an exercise for the user. As a fork of bolt.diy adapted for Convex, Chef generates full-stack applications whose data layer, authentication, file storage, real-time subscriptions, and scheduled functions are wired to Convex's reactive database from the start, so the output is a functioning product rather than a mockup. It runs hosted at chef.convex.dev with a free tier, and the codebase — including its system prompt, published openly — can be self-hosted for teams that want control. The builder targets founders and product engineers who want working backends (not just UI) from a prompt, and it doubles as a showcase for Convex as a backend-as-a-service platform. It is under active development by the Convex team with a growing open-source community.
