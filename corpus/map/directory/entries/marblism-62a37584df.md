Marblism packages business automation as personnel rather than software: subscribers hire Eva for inbox and calendar, Sonny for social media, Stan for lead generation backed by a large contact database, Walter for website building, Penny for SEO content, Rachel for phone reception, and Linda for legal drafting. The agents connect to the customer's existing Gmail, Outlook, and social accounts, act on ongoing instructions, and improve with feedback, targeting small businesses that cannot staff these roles. One flat subscription (roughly $24-44/month by billing term) covers all workers with about 50 hours of monthly work and a 7-day money-back guarantee. Only Walter's website building touches software creation; the platform is business-process automation.
